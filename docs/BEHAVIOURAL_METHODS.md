# Behavioural implementation and source contracts

This module implements prospective analysis of the supplied proposal. It does not contain biological findings. Biological source files are acquired on Narval; local tests contain synthetic fixtures only.

## Source identity and chronological time

`discover_fly_sources(directory, root, config=None)` inventories native author `*_margoConvert.mat` files paired with survival workbooks. It returns `fly_sources`, unresolved schemas and per-source exposure readiness. The companion page explicitly maps `Fig 4 + Supp` to classifier experiments. Other assays require their own inspected geometry, condition/control and family annotations.

The author `expmt.Centroid.data` stores time × coordinate × ROI. Survival rows map to ROI indices under the author’s verified `1:height(survival)` convention. Canonical animal identity is experiment plus native ROI. Native `expmt.Time.data` contains inter-frame intervals in seconds; the adapter cumulatively sums these and adds the filename’s recording-start clock. This interpretation follows the author’s `convertMargo.m` and MARGO `autoTrack.m` at commit `e9d9d0756d3bb18c1a837622ecd5a4e180806012`. Other clock arrays require explicit units. A nominal frame-rate clock is disabled unless explicitly configured with provenance.

Classifier ROI bounds are native `[x,y,width,height]` rectangles. They supply the arena extent, rather than future trajectory minima/maxima. Source `mm_per_pix`, when present, supplies physical scale; otherwise coordinates remain normalized arena units. Image-y is reversed and the author’s standard food-bottom arena is recorded with source provenance. Foods, sides and geometries from unrelated assays are not borrowed. Pooled `allMatrix` products normalized with whole-trajectory extrema and death-aligned `toDmatrix` products are ineligible forecasting inputs.

`iter_fly_source` emits one identified animal at a time. `run(..., stage='ingest')` writes native Parquet row groups and one-second-derived state bins at the primary 10 seconds and 5/20-second sensitivity resolutions. It holds one experiment’s MAT arrays and one ROI frame instead of all experiments’ raw coordinate tables. Complete content hashes collapse identical duplicate representations; conflicting duplicates fail. Native/bin content hashes bind subsequent analysis. Source clocks, geometry, annotations and missingness remain available for audit.

Short gaps use both-bound interpolation only when the right bound has become available. Leading/trailing partial bins and bins with >20% missing one-second observations are masked. Missing state bins preserve the regular clock. Predictors use previous position/displacement, circadian phase, missingness and the observed food/arena geometry. Eventual death, survival, sporulation and fictive death are prohibited predictors. They remain separate annotations.

The primary modulation is `elapsed_exposure_hours`. It requires original 0/1 treatment/exposure assignment plus `exposure_elapsed_at_start_hours` and exposure-clock provenance. Unexposed animals receive zero modulation. The source adapter will not interpret survival as lack of exposure or invent an exposure offset from a `Day3` filename. Hardware pilots explicitly use elapsed recording time and cannot establish infection effects.

The classifier publication describes 1,306 exposed training flies, whereas the deposited native files contain full 128-ROI trays. The author [`load_data_utils.py`](https://github.com/dlavrent/e_muscae_summit_classifier/blob/1a9b14facfac04c81d31f801c0d387ea64b71555/utils/load_data_utils.py) and [`process_data_utils.py`](https://github.com/dlavrent/e_muscae_summit_classifier/blob/1a9b14facfac04c81d31f801c0d387ea64b71555/utils/process_data_utils.py) select survivor/cadaver records and exclude NI or incompletely annotated cadavers; they provide no exposure timestamps. A statement about that selected classifier subset cannot assign exposure to every raw ROI. The 14 October 2026 source amendment therefore retains blank `Rx` as unknown instead of automatically setting every native tray row to exposed. Earlier derived files retain their original provenance and must be regenerated before a formal exposure analysis uses this amendment. The generic assay Methods also describe non-exposed controls and a 48–72-hour loading interval, rather than exact experiment-specific exposure-start events ([article Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10259475/)). Required evidence is an original experiment/ROI treatment assignment and a recording-to-exposure offset with clock provenance; neither an eventual outcome nor a nominal day label replaces that evidence. The audit reports exposed, unexposed and unresolved animal counts explicitly.

## Deposited quantitative source replication

`replicate_source_summit` repeats the deposited author `integrateSummitBump.m` calculation using the original death-aligned speed/height arrays and stored parameters. These descriptive source checks do not supply prediction features or new independent observations. Original source identities stored as MATLAB strings are decoded from the inspected object representation; unsupported serializations stay unresolved. Pairing accepts a unique original author basename or publication-associated basename, including the archive's `Cs_tallwells_30deg.mat.mat` extension. It requires matching source-version stamps, cadaver counts and per-cadaver metric dimensions before comparing numbers. It never truncates a matrix, creates missing columns or splits mixed-sex arrays to fit expected results.

The 14 October native inspection found a substantive source-version gap: `Cs_30deg_food0_QUANT.mat` describes 89 cadavers from `2020-6-29_19_58_47`, while the deposited `Cs_30deg_food0.mat` contains 69 cadaver columns and was generated at `2021-6-29_14_9_0`. The female and male QUANT files describe 297 and 258 cadavers and refer to original `Cs WF_Female_last12 hours.mat` and `Cs WF_Male_last12 hours.mat` sources from July 2022. Those sex-specific source files are absent from the inspected archive; the mixed-sex pooled file contains no per-cadaver sex crosswalk. Matching 297 + 258 to the pooled count of 555 cannot identify the original column subsets. These gaps require the original corresponding source versions. The double-extension tall-wells file is a resolvable archive naming issue; successful numerical replication still requires an actual scheduled run.

Amended replication uses an explicit fresh `output_dir` under personal study storage, optionally an independently curated `source_directory` containing hash-bound source amendments. Existing output directories are refused before analysis, preserving earlier comparison tables and receipts. Every inspected quantitative file and paired data file, including unsuccessful pairs, is included in the new content-hash record. Native inspection evidence, newly executed replication and older benchmarks retain their distinct source identities.

## Models and validation

`semimarkov.Sequence` carries an independent animal/experiment, chronological observations, current/past context, missingness and bin duration. Scaling is fitted on training animals only, giving each animal equal contribution to its feature moments. Held-out animals and experiments cannot enter optimization or duration-knot selection.

The primary two-state duration semi-Markov model uses multivariate Student-t emissions with five degrees of freedom. Ordered rest/moving noise scales fix the primary state ordering. Exit hazards depend on context, log dwell time, a restricted cubic duration basis with three training-only quartile knots and noncentered animal/experiment effects. Moving drift combines previous displacement, food and arena-axis terms. MG modulates moving means and covariance together; MT modulates transition hazards; MD rotates drift while preserving its norm; MX combines these. M0 omits the modulation terms. Optional three-state fits require separate stability/identifiability evidence. The forward recursion normalizes duration/state occupancy and integrates missing observations rather than deleting elapsed time.

Exact duration recursion grows with sequence length and can be expensive. A finite duration cap uses an aggregated geometric tail, never a forced exit, and formal fits reject posterior tail mass above their configured tolerance. Capped pilot timing is not evidence that the exact full-tail likelihood or complete recovery workload fits the study budget.

MAP optimization uses matched Adam learning-rate grids, clipping, early validation stopping, five starts and deterministic L-BFGS refinement. Nearly inconsistent semi-Markov starts can be expanded to 20. Prior and per-animal likelihood gradients are accumulated exactly, releasing each animal’s graph after backward; a test checks equality against a single complete objective. No truncated temporal gradient replaces the semi-Markov likelihood. New-animal/experiment scoring integrates random-effect uncertainty using fixed Gaussian quadrature.

Baselines include a geometric-duration HMM, a regularized autoregressive Student-t predictor and a one-layer GRU. The GRU uses 60 preceding state bins plus missingness, a Student-t head, widths 16/32/64 and dropout 0/0.1, under a 100,000-parameter cap. Current targets and future observations never enter history. The same grouped validation and optimizer budget apply. Comparator simulation updates its own history and position causally.

`nested_validation` holds out complete experiments and animals, selects hyperparameters in grouped inner folds, then scores independent outer animals after a 600-second warm-up. Experiments receive equal weight. Array jobs write per-fold TSVs; `aggregate_outer_folds` requires every declared fold, checks identities and rejects repeated animal/model predictions. H1 selects MT versus MX from inner scores only. Changing outer scores cannot change that selection.

`diagnose_outer_folds` uses each outer fold’s training animals to fix movement threshold and component scales, then generates held-out trajectories with frozen fits. It compares bout restricted mean duration, occupancy and conditional speed. The observation likelihood additionally records marginal 90% prediction coverage. Generated trajectories are not physically clipped to arena walls; failure to reproduce spatial behavior is a model failure to diagnose, not a repaired biological observation.

`recovery_analysis` uses original clocks/missingness and known MG/MT/MD/MX generating effects 0/.25/.5/1 with `parameter_recovery_replicates=100`. It refits grouped candidate models and exports confusion and parameter estimates. Confusion alone does not establish parameter bias/coverage, numerical parameter separation or posterior adequacy. These additional identifiability checks remain required before a positive biological claim. `bayesian_check` implements subset preconditioned MALA with explicit acceptance, split-Rhat and ESS checks; poor mixing cannot validate MAP evidence.

## Development freeze and intervention predictions

`run(..., stage='freeze-development')` requires complete inner selection for every development experiment, chooses the selective candidate on inner validation, refits MG/MT/MX/GRU on development animals only, and writes `manifests/wp1_development_freeze.json`. It prospectively defines movement as displacement speed above the development positive-speed median and freezes empirical component standard deviations. These operational definitions are auditable modeling choices. Targeted animal identities, amplitudes and withheld component outcomes do not choose them.

`resolve_intervention_settings` loads that unchanged config-bound freeze and `manifests/intervention_adapters.json` only when the latter has `audit_status='validated_source_crosswalk'` and hashes of the original crosswalk evidence. Reagent names or pooled matrix column indices are not biological IDs. Readiness records list absent animal/condition/control/family annotations explicitly.

Each intervention/control has a deterministic animal hash split. Reused controls retain identical IDs and halves. One amplitude is calibrated from integrated activity in one half; entry, persistence, occupancy and conditional speed are evaluated in the other, then halves reverse. MG and selective directions come from frozen development coefficients. The unrestricted GRU shifts its frozen learned progression-context response with one amplitude, allowing nonlinear predictive dynamics rather than giving it a second gain-only model. No intervention animal may enter WP1 training.

H2 resamples shared condition/experiment/half animals once per draw and reuses that draw across contrasts and half reversal. Contrast units then enter the primary comparison. Calibration-response derivatives propagate amplitude uncertainty conditionally on frozen WP1 fits. Coarse/fine derivatives and the resampling response radius are checked; a local approximation failing these checks remains provisional and cannot pass H2’s operational gate. Full nonlocal recalibration, if required by actual data, remains pending rather than being replaced with a fictitious exact interval. A separate family-held-out refitting analysis is not implied by merely writing family fold indices.

Screen effect estimation performs shared-control-aware animal/experiment resampling. GLS groups reagents by validated source gene IDs, adds estimated excess reagent variance and projects the complete reagent covariance to gene-level covariance, including cross-gene shared controls. Gene export requires the outcome-blind molecular freeze, its unchanged independent FlyBase catalog and orthogroup map. It writes gene effects, covariance, ordered IDs and full hashes. Conditional cadaver/source inclusion is retained and limits causal interpretation.

## Published mantis adapters

The polarization adapter identifies only the unique laboratory Data S2 sheet in the public Dryad workbook (DOI `10.5061/dryad.jh9w0vtkb`). It reads source individual/date/trial IDs, infection, degree of polarization, box, year, side and illuminance. Native `result` categories `hori-pola`, `unpola` and `M` are retained as polarized, unpolarized and nonchoice. Binary `result_hori` cannot replace the three-category response. Source polarization units are preserved instead of inferred by magnitude. The model fits engagement and conditional preference, an infection-by-polarization term, relevant context and a repeated-individual intercept; a three-category sensitivity model retains nonchoices. Individual/date-held-out predictive validation for these secondary choice models remains a separate required analysis, not a claimed result of fitting the complete table.

The 13 October 2026 parser/statistical repair is an explicit amendment made after inspecting source coding and before interpreting fitted effects. All 206 deposited observations remain in the canonical table. Five response/location/window disagreements and one nonchoice/binary-response disagreement are flagged without changing raw annotations. The conditional analysis uses the 200 mutually consistent rows; separate all-row recorded-response and location/window alternatives are diagnostic sensitivity analyses. Successful fitting cannot reconcile these six source ambiguities or establish an unqualified author replication.

The source context has a nuisance alias: all 2021 observations used box D, so `Intercept = year_2022 + year_2023 + box_D`. Both the full and consistent-row engagement/chooser designs have 13 columns and rank 12. Their likelihood nullspace has zero infection, polarization and infection-by-polarization components, establishing that these protected coefficients remain estimable. Only redundant nuisance columns may be removed; primary confounding produces explicit pending status and descriptive counts without a coefficient claim.

The reduction preserves the entire predictor column space. Writing the original design as `X = Z A` gives `X beta = Z gamma`, where `gamma = A beta`. The original `beta ~ Normal(0, 2.25 I)` prior induces `gamma ~ Normal(0, 2.25 A Aᵀ)`; this covariance, rather than a new independent prior on the retained nuisance columns, enters the MAP objective. Scaling and basis selection use training observations only. Held-out original covariates must equal the retained covariates multiplied by the training transform, for both engagement and conditional preference. A new year/box combination that violates a training alias remains outside identifiable training contexts and is reported as pending. Three-category prediction checks this support for every test row, without using the observed response to hide unsupported conditional predictions. Design, alias, prior and pending-status evidence is retained in `mantis_statistical_design_audit.json`.

Binary hurdle and multinomial sensitivity MAP fits use exact analytic gradients of the existing likelihood and induced-prior objectives, including repeated-individual noncentered intercepts and their scale prior. BFGS, convergence tolerances and iteration limits are unchanged. Central finite differences check both gradients, and synthetic fits verify agreement with the previous finite-difference objectives and predictions. Missing held-out categorical support and changes to an omitted constant training covariate are checked before optimization; full engagement and chooser-specific training-basis guards still follow fitting.

The mating adapter follows the actual CSV/README (DOI `10.5061/dryad.4j0zpc8jw`): infection/event indicators are 0/1, first-event latency is in minutes and censor 1 means right-censored at `obs_time`. Censored latencies use survival probability, never zero event times. The escape-versus-mounting male-infection contrast is directly estimated with pair/connected-reuse cluster bootstrap when individual IDs exist. The public README’s pair records lack verified male/female identifiers; source-hash/row keys provide provenance but do not establish biological independence. Such uncertainty is explicitly conditional on unresolved individual reuse.

## Claim and figure artifacts

H1/H2 writers emit actual estimates, CI and raw P only when required independent contrasts exist. They record config/source/data-manifest hashes and the absolute paths plus hashes of every plotted/analyzed result. Figure inputs 02/03 come from these actual tables. H1’s operational gate requires parameter identifiability, gain-only recovery and >=5% multivariate diagnostic improvement. H2 requires >=10% improvement against both gain and unrestricted references, benefits across at least two families and validated calibration uncertainty. The parent report performs the joint H1/H2/H3 Holm adjustment. Missing diagnostics are recorded as pending, not converted to numerical success.

Behavioural hazards, gain and drift cannot identify signs in neural input/recurrent/hidden-state/output parameter bases. `export_parameter_readiness` exports actual training-only behavioural coefficients and split/hash provenance to `biological_direction_readiness.json`, leaving unavailable neural classes empty. An explicit prospective bridge and development-only signature calibration are needed for biological transfer. The separate synthetic neural causal experiment can proceed without asserting identified biological neural directions.

## Callable stages

All stages use `run(config: dict, root: pathlib.Path, stage: str, **kwargs)`; the parent CLI supplies configuration and root. Behaviour stages: `ingest`, `validate`, `mantis`, `pilot`, `split`, `reproduce`, `fit`, `aggregate-folds`, `posterior-checks`, `recover`, `bayesian-check`, `freeze-development`, `export-parameters`, `inference`. `fit`/`posterior-checks` accept `outer_experiment`; primary/sensitivity bins accept `bin_seconds`. `reproduce` currently writes chronological summaries/splits; author summit-metric reproduction is explicitly separate and pending original source criteria/IDs.

Intervention stages: `readiness`, `screen`, `challenge`, `inference`. Frozen fits, threshold and scales auto-load from the development manifest; source annotations auto-load only from audited crosswalks. `pilot` measures two native development experiments, fixed outcome-independent hashed animal IDs (at most four per experiment) and the first 720 bins with one start/10 epochs and an explicitly capped timing model. It retains full membership/native bin counts, native input/config/source/Slurm/CPU/RAM/storage provenance and marks complete-study admission pending full-tail, fit and 100-replicate recovery measurements.
